# Sarah Borni

### PhD Student in Structural Biology | Weizmann Institute of Science

Welcome to my academic website! I am a PhD student in [Prof. Ron Diskin's laboratory](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/csb/diskin/welcome-letter) at the Weizmann Institute of Science, where I study viral structural biology and antibody-mediated immunity.

## About Me

I completed my M.Sc. in Structural Biology at the Weizmann Institute of Science and continued my academic journey as a PhD student.

My scientific interests include structural biology, virology, cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM), and the development of strategies to understand and prevent viral infections.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on the structure and function of viral proteins, with a particular interest in the Lujo virus spike protein.


## Academic Achievements

- Completed an M.Sc. in Structural Biology at the Weizmann Institute of Science.
- Contributed to research on the structure of the Lujo virus spike complex.
- Presented my research at the ILANIT/FISEB conference.
- Participated in a Three Minute Thesis (3MT) competition.

## Research Skills

- **Structural biology:** Cryo-EM data collection and structural visualization.
- **Molecular biology:** Cloning, mutagenesis, DNA and protein purification.
- **Protein analysis:** Western blotting, ELISA and protein purification using FPLC.
- **Cell biology:** Mammalian cell culture, transfection and neutralization assays.
- **Data analysis:** Flow cytometry analysis and molecular structure visualization using PyMOL.

## Future Plans

During my PhD, I aim to deepen my understanding of viral structural biology and contribute to research that advances our knowledge of viral infections and potential approaches to prevention.

I also hope to continue developing my scientific communication skills and sharing research with broader audiences.
